# Demand Farm 首发与获客文案

## 核心定位

一句话：

> Demand Farm 是 zyagent 的收入实验室，从近期公开信号中找出真实痛点、
> 购买意愿、定价实验和内容机会。证据不足时，它会直接告诉你。

不要宣传“万能 Agent”。统一宣传“减少错误产品决策”和“形成可测试报价”。

## GitHub / Indie Hackers 首发

标题：

> I built an evidence-first demand researcher because AI idea generators are too confident

正文：

> Most AI product tools can generate ten startup ideas in seconds. The problem is that they
> rarely tell you whether anyone is complaining, switching, paying, or even discussing the
> problem.
>
> I added Demand Farm to zyagent, an open-source local agent. You enter a market, customer,
> and goal. It searches recent Hacker News discussions, GitHub issues, and Google News,
> then produces pain points, buying signals, counter-signals, an MVP, pricing experiments,
> and interview steps with source links.
>
> The first real test was intentionally disappointing: "AI receptionist for dental clinics"
> returned mostly vendor content and no direct buyer evidence. Instead of inventing
> confidence, the report said the evidence was weak and recommended customer interviews.
> That honesty is the feature.
>
> It runs locally with your own model key, has tool-call review, conversation branching,
> context compression, and HTML/Markdown exports.
>
> GitHub: https://github.com/wanname777/zyagent
> Demo: https://wanname777.github.io/zyagent/

## 首批访谈私信

> 我在做一个开源的需求研究 Agent。它不会只生成点子，而是从近期公开讨论里找痛点、
> 购买和替代信号，证据不足就明确说不足。
>
> 我正在找 5 位独立开发者或自动化顾问做 20 分钟测试。你给我一个正在考虑的市场，
> 我免费生成一份初步报告；作为交换，只想知道哪些结论能改变你的决策、哪些完全没用。
>
> 如果结果有价值，后续完整报告定价 99 美元。没有销售压力。

## 99 美元报告交付范围

- 一个明确市场和目标客户
- 最近 30 天公开信号
- 痛点、购买信号、反信号和证据缺口
- 竞争空档和最小可售卖方案
- 三个价格实验
- 三个内容获客角度
- 五个访谈问题
- 一次 30 分钟结果讨论

不承诺市场规模、收入或成功概率，不出售未经验证的联系人。
